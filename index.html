How traders read these

  • Up/Down 4% (Stockbee) close ±4% vs prior close, volume ≥ 100k and above the prior day. Universe: all US common stocks, no price filter, as in his TC2000 scan. 300+ up-4% in a day = breadth thrust; 300+ down-4% = heavy selling.
  • 5-day / 10-day ratio sum of up-4% ÷ sum of down-4%. 10-day ratio above 2 = strong, below 0.5 = weak/washout (often near lows).
  • 25% up/down in a quarter (Stockbee) close vs lowest/highest close of 65 days, 20d avg close × avg volume ≥ $250k. Intermediate trend gauge; when the "down" count exceeds the "up" count the market is in a correction. Extremes of 200 or fewer up stocks often mark lows.
  • 25% / 50% in a month (Stockbee) close vs close 20 days ago (that close ≥ $5, $250k liquidity). 13% in 34 days close vs lowest/highest close of 34 days. Short-term momentum; rising up-counts show momentum expanding; spikes in down-counts show capitulation.
  • T2108 % of all stocks in the universe above their 40-day MA (Worden indicator). Below 20 = oversold, above 70 = overbought.
  • Classic indicators below (%>MA, A/D, NH/NL, McClellan, up/down volume, Zweig) use the core subset: close ≥ $5 and 50-day avg volume ≥ 100k.
  • % above 20/50/200-day MA above 50% = majority in uptrends; above 80 overbought, below 20 oversold.
  • New highs / new lows (52-week) NH−NL positive and expanding confirms a healthy advance. High-Low Index = 10-day MA of NH/(NH+NL); above 50 bullish.
  • A/D line cumulative advances minus declines; divergence from the index warns of narrowing participation.
  • McClellan Oscillator / Summation 19 vs 39-day EMA of ratio-adjusted net advances (×1000). Above 0 = bullish breadth momentum; readings beyond ±100 are extremes. Summation = running total (trend).
  • Up/Down volume dollar volume in advancers ÷ decliners; 9:1 days either way mark thrusts/capitulation.
  • Zweig Breadth Thrust 10-day EMA of adv/(adv+dec) moving from below 40% to above 61.5% within 10 days, a rare and bullish signal.
  • Regime rule (transparent) one point each for: 10d ratio ≥ 1.5, T2108 ≥ 50, %>50D ≥ 50, %>200D ≥ 50, McClellan > 0, NH−NL > 0. 5–6 = Bullish, 2–4 = Neutral, 0–1 = Bearish.

Gappers ⚡ (pre-market)

Loading…

How to trade it (plain English). 1) Don't buy at the open bell. Wait for the first 5 minutes to finish. 2) Mark the high and low of that first 5-minute candle (the "opening range"). 3) Gap-up: buy only if price breaks above the 5-min high and stays above VWAP. Stop goes at the 5-min low. 4) If it loses VWAP, get out. 5) Use small size, since gappers move fast. Gap-downs: the mirror image (below the 5-min low, under VWAP), for experienced traders only. Education, not advice.

Methodology

  • Universe AI watchlist (stocks and ETFs) + S&P 500 + Nasdaq-100 + S&P 400. Non-watchlist names require price > $5 and 50-day average dollar volume > $20M. Fundamentals via Finviz; daily OHLCV via Yahoo Finance.
  • Tight range 20-day high–low range ≤ max(12%, 3.5 × ATR14%) and Bollinger width in the bottom 25% of its 1-year history or ATR(14)% down 15%+ vs 20 days ago. Also tracked: NR7, inside days, volume dry-up.
  • 8/21 EMA 8-day vs 21-day EMA; “fresh” = crossed within the last 5 sessions.
  • Higher lows each of the last 3 weekly lows above the prior one. 10/40-week MA close above the 10- and 40-week SMAs.
  • Breakout close above the prior 20-day high on volume ≥ 1.5× the 50-day average. Retest broke a 20-day high in the last 10 sessions, pulled back within 3% of it, and still closes above.
  • RVOL day volume / prior 50-day (and 20-day) average; 3-day average for accumulation. ≥ 1.5 on an up day in the top third = breakout volume; ≥ 2 = strong; 3-day < 0.7 = base dry-up.
  • Trigger / Stop trigger = prior 20-day high (next resistance if already above); stop = 10-day low or base low. Stop dist. = (trigger − stop) / trigger.
  • Scores Value (0–100): forward P/E vs sector, PEG, P/S, EV/EBITDA, FCF yield, target upside, % off 52-week high. Setup (0–100): EMA state, higher lows, MAs, tight range, dry-up, NR7/inside, breakout, retest, RVOL, distance to trigger. Combined = 0.4 × Value + 0.6 × Setup.
  • Kissing MA rising 50D/100D/200D SMA or 40-week SMA, price 0–3% above it (or tagged it within 1% and held), plus a tight range.
  • All-time high uses each stock's full Yahoo price history; breakout requires RVOL ≥ 1.5 and a top-third close. Major levels 52-week high, 60/120-day base highs (≥ 15 prior closes under), round numbers.
  • Top picks rule setup ≥ 60, within 4% of trigger, stop distance ≥ 1.5%, ranked by combined score (top 8), plus the 2 best AI names.
